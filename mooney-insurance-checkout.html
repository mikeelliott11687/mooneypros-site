Mooney insurance checkout training

If your underwriter requires dual instruction in your Mooney before you can fly solo or carry passengers, a Mooney Pros instructor will fly those hours with you in your airplane. We help new owners, pilots moving up to a faster or turbocharged Mooney, and pilots coming back after time away.

Insurance underwriters typically specify a training time required before solo flight or carrying passengers. Our instructors’ evaluation and sign-off, though, depend solely on the competency and safety of the pilot — not on the clock alone. Read the training requirement in your policy or quote, and send it to us. We’ll build the training around it.

How it works

  1. Send us your policy’s training requirement. That means the dual hours, any make-and-model time, and any limits on passengers.
  2. Add Mooney Pros, Inc. and your assigned instructor to your policy as named additional insureds with a waiver of subrogation. Your agent handles this. We need the certificate before any flying starts.
  3. Train. We fly the hours as real Mooney training, not laps around the patch: systems, landings, emergencies, and a cross-country.
  4. Get your logbook endorsement and the documentation your underwriter asks for.

Why the insurance requirement matters to you

We’re a flight training company, not an insurance company. The additional-insured requirement keeps everyone on the same side of the table if something ever goes wrong, and it’s standard for us on every flight. Our instructors also follow a syllabus that’s on file with at least one major aviation insurance broker.

We don’t promise a premium discount.
