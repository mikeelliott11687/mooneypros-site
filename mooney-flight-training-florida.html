Mooney flight training in Florida

Mooney Pros is based in Tarpon Springs, Florida, in the Tampa Bay area. We provide Mooney transition training, insurance checkouts, flight reviews, and IPCs at your airport anywhere in Florida. We fly from your home field in your own airplane, so you train in the airspace and weather you actually fly in.

Florida-based instructors

  • Mike Elliott lives in Tarpon Springs and has been active in the Tampa Bay flying community since moving to Florida in 2012.
  • Rob McGuire is based in Hawthorne, Florida, near Gainesville, and will fly to your location.
  • Other Mooney Pros instructors travel to Florida regularly. See all instructors →

What Florida Mooney pilots ask us for

  • Transition training after buying a Mooney
  • Insurance checkout hours before going solo or carrying passengers
  • Flight reviews and IPCs with WINGS credit
  • Repositioning flights into or out of Florida

Mooney Summit in Tampa

Florida is also home to the Mooney Summit, the annual Mooney safety and education event Mike co-founded. Mooney Summit XII is September 23–25, 2027, in Tampa.
