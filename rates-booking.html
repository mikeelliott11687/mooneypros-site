Mooney training rates and how booking works

Mooney Pros charges $800 per training day and $500 per half day. An instructor travel day is $800, or $500 when it’s a half day. The instructor’s direct expenses, such as airfare, hotel, meals, and ground transport, are paid directly to the instructor, and flights and hotels are prepaid to the instructor before training starts.

Rates

Item Rate
Training day $800
Half day $500
Instructor travel day $800, or $500 for a half day
Instructor direct expenses Paid directly to the instructor at cost
Airfare and hotel Prepaid to the instructor before training

A trip with 2 training days and 2 instructor travel days:

Line Days Rate Amount

Plus the instructor’s direct expenses, which are paid directly to the instructor. Airfare and hotel are prepaid to the instructor before training.

How billing works

  • Mooney Pros, Inc. invoices you for training days and travel days at the end of your session. Payment is due on receipt.
  • Your instructor sends you their direct-expense accounting, and you pay them directly.
  • Full terms are in the training agreement you sign before scheduling. Past-due balances are charged 1.5% per month.

The insurance requirement

Before any flying, your aircraft insurance has to name Mooney Pros, Inc. and your assigned instructor as named additional insureds, with a waiver of subrogation. Your agent can usually do this quickly. Have them email the certificate to mike@mooneypros.com. It protects you, your instructor, and the company, and keeps everyone on the same side of the table.

How to book

  1. Contact us with your Mooney model, tail number, home airport, dates, and goals (and your policy’s training requirement, if you have one). Contact →
  2. We match you with an instructor and confirm dates and an estimate.
  3. Sign the training agreement online. Training Request & Service Agreement →
  4. Send the insurance certificate, and prepay the instructor’s airfare and hotel.
  5. Fly.
